# Worked examples

Hook suggestions alone are not enough. After each finished step, produce examples with real content.

## Before you start
First make sure the data map is built and up to date (`data-map` skill) and the user has seen it; examples come after the map.
Then tell the user: for good inspiration quality use a strong model (for example Sonnet or Opus, or another large model), not a small one like Haiku. You cannot switch the model yourself. Continue only after the user has had the chance to switch.

## Idea freshness rule (applies before every batch of examples)
Lesson from testing: the first examples only re-skinned what the creator already posts (same topics, same tools, same hooks) and the creator rejected them as not new. Prevent this every time:
1. **Build the "already covered" list first** from the vault: the creator's own top and bottom videos, their topics, tools and recurring phrases, and their past hooks. Read it before generating anything.
2. **Never re-skin.** A new idea must NOT be the same topic with a new hook, a "part 2", a recap, or a step-by-step of something the creator already explained. A follow-up is allowed only if the audience asked for it (comments) and you say so.
3. **Idea seeds first, scripts second.** Start with a menu of 5 genuinely new seeds (one line each: hook, why it is new, risk), then write the full script only for the one the creator picks. Do not write full scripts for ideas the creator has not chosen.
4. **Mix of new idea types**, at most 1 of 5 may stay inside the creator's existing topics: an experiment or test the creator runs (their results shown as they come out, never pre-written), a challenge or bet, a role swap (a real beginner does it), a series built on a real local problem, an audience-decides format, a confession or contrarian view, a reversal of the creator's usual role. Use the creator's gaps from the landscape (`gaps.md`) as raw material.
5. **Self-check before showing:** for each seed ask "could this have been made from the creator's last 20 videos?" If yes, replace it. Say plainly that you did not verify whether others already cover it.
6. **Experiments are not scripts.** For test/challenge ideas never write the outcome; leave the result slots empty and let the creator fill them with what really happens.
7. If the creator says the ideas are recycled or not new, do not defend them: ask what is missing, add it to the "already covered" list, and regenerate the whole menu.

## Each example contains
1. Idea, pattern (from the strategy) and why it fits the creator's angle.
2. **Spoken script** in the creator's language, dialect and voice (use their voice profile), with the hook in the first seconds, then the points in order, then the call to action. Say what is demonstrated on screen as content; do not give filming or editing instructions.
3. **Caption and hashtags** in the creator's style.
4. **Keyword and resource** if the video has a keyword call to action; offer to write the resource.
5. **Verification checklist:** every factual claim (feature names, shortcuts, prices, paths) the creator should test on their own machine before filming. Ground examples in things the creator actually has or does; never invent numbers, prices or results; flag what you are unsure about. If the creator reports a claim did not work, fix it and note the lesson.
6. Keep the creator's off-limits and the inspiration rule: new idea, own experience.

## After the examples, always ask
"Is this content enough, or should I make it longer and deeper?" Adapt length and depth to the answer, and ask which idea to develop next.
Give 1-2 full examples at a time rather than many short ones, always chosen from a fresh-idea menu (see the freshness rule).
